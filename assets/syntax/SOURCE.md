# Greek NT syntax trees (served to the Bible Study Genius app)

`<book>.json` files are compact conversions of the MACULA Greek "lowfat" syntax
trees for the Nestle 1904 Greek New Testament
(https://github.com/Clear-Bible/macula-greek, commit 8423afe), made by
`scripts/build-syntax.js` in the app repo (`biblegenius-build1`).

- MACULA Greek © Biblica, Inc. — CC BY 4.0 (attribution shown in the app).
- Nestle 1904 text: public domain; morphology CC0; Berean Interlinear glosses: public domain.

The app downloads a book the first time it's opened and caches it on the device.
1 John, Philemon and Ephesians ship inside the app and aren't here.
